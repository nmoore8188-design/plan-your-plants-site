Plan Your Plants

A plant journal for your iPhone. Keep track of your plants, where they live, when you watered, how the soil felt, and how they're growing, with photos along the way.

Gentle reminders nudge you to check the soil, and an optional outside forecast gives a heads-up about frost, heat, and heavy rain.

No account, no sign-in, no ads. Your plants stay on your phone.
