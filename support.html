FAQ

How do I back up my plants?

On the home screen, tap Export, then Back up everything. You'll get one ZIP file with your plants, spots, care logs, readings, original photos, and a report you can read. Save it to Files, iCloud Drive, or wherever you like.

Not in the backup: reminder times, quiet hours, and your weather place. You'll set those again after a restore.

How do I restore a backup or move to a new phone?

Get the backup ZIP onto the phone (Files or iCloud Drive works), open the app, go to Export, and tap Restore from a backup. Pick the ZIP, check the preview, then tap Replace everything on this phone.

Restoring replaces what's in the app now, so back up first if you want to keep it. If a restore can't finish, the app tries to put things back the way they were.

I'm not getting reminders.

  • In the app, open Reminders and tap Allow reminders if you see it.
  • Check iPhone Settings > Notifications > Plan Your Plants and make sure notifications are on.
  • Check the quiet hours in the app's Reminders screen, and any Focus mode on your phone.
  • Reminders only show up when something's due. If nothing needs a look, you won't get one.

Can I hide plant names on notifications and the widget?

Yes. In Reminders, turn on Hide plant names.

Weather isn't showing.

Weather needs either location permission or a city you type in, plus an internet connection. You can switch location on in iPhone Settings > Plan Your Plants > Location, or just enter a city in the app instead.

How do I delete my data?

Delete single plants, photos, or readings inside the app. To erase everything, delete the app from your iPhone. Your data only lives on your phone, so that removes it. Exported backup ZIPs are separate files you can delete yourself.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in, and your plants stay on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
